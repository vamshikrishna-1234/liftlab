How it works

Liftlab connects read-only to your BigQuery, Snowflake, or Redshift, runs SQL templates inside the warehouse, and ships an executive report. Try the live version below — it runs end-to-end on synthetic data in about seven seconds.

Step 1

Stratified TVC split

90/10 (configurable) test/control split balanced across customer segment, persona, channel mix, and division. Falls back to random for tiny strata. Includes a post-hoc balance verification.

Step 2

Engagement & ops integrity

Per-channel send / open / click / unsub aggregation. Validates that the test side actually got the comm and the control hold-out stayed clean — the check that catches broken campaigns before the wrong number ships.

Step 3

Incrementality + significance

Per-HH lift %, incremental net sales / units / visits, Welch's two-sample t-tests for significance, segment-level breakdowns across every customer dimension.

Step 4

Auto-narrated executive summary

Headline numbers, channel performance, best/worst segments with p-values, operational integrity, and recommended next experiments — generated from the actual analysis output.

Step 5

Multi-sheet Excel deliverable

Executive Summary, Incrementality, Engagement, Daily Engagement, Ops Efficiency, TVC Balance, plus one sheet per segment dimension. Drop-in-ready for your existing reporting template.

Step 6

Warehouse-native

SQL templates run inside your BigQuery / Snowflake / Redshift. No data movement, no separate cluster, no compliance review. Read-only, audit-logged.
